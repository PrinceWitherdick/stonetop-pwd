`spring.svg` (used by the Seasons Change macro) is the project's own seasonal flower icon, and
`arcanum.svg` is the project's own redrawing of the triple spiral the rulebooks stamp beside an
arcanum — a larger-marked copy of `assets/icons/arcanum.svg`. Neither is a game-icons.net asset,
so neither is listed above.

`arcanum.svg` and `animal-skull.svg` are kept although no macro carries them any more: they
belonged to "Browse the Arcana" and "Browse the Bestiary", which merged into the one "Browse
Stonetop" macro on `magnifying-glass.svg`.
