# Stonetop for Foundry VTT

An unofficial Foundry VTT system for playing [Stonetop](https://plusoneexp.com/collections/stonetop), the pastoral fantasy game by Jeremy Strandberg. It takes the bookkeeping off the table: every playbook as a rollable sheet, guided character creation and leveling, followers, the steading and its seasons, fights without initiative, expeditions, a bestiary, the campaign's timeline and more.

This wiki covers what the system does and how to use it, including a lot that's easy to miss. If you only read one page, read [Hidden Tricks](Hidden-Tricks).

## Ten things most people miss

1. **Hold `Shift` while you click a roll** to skip every question and roll straight away.
2. **Left-click your token in a fight** for Clash, Let Fly, Defend and your damage right on the map.
3. **Drag your row onto a foe's on the Fight tab** and your token steps up next to them.
4. **Your Preferences tab** (last tab on your own sheet) has dark modes, bigger text, high contrast and no-italics, for your screen only.
5. **Drag a move onto the hotbar** to roll it with your sheet closed.
6. **Drag an NPC or monster onto your sheet** to make it your follower.
7. **The rulebooks open inside Foundry**, with your own bookmarks, and page citations that jump to the rule.
8. **The weather, season and year bar** at the top of the screen is the GM's control panel for the calendar.
9. **The relationship map** takes `Alt`-drag to draw a line and `Ctrl`+`Z` to undo your own changes.
10. **A miss marks your XP for you**, with an **Undo** if it shouldn't have.
